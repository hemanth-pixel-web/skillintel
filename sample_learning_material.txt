Official statistics are produced to provide trusted evidence for society and policy.
Data quality includes accuracy, consistency, coherence, relevance, timeliness and accessibility.
A sampling frame is a list or source that identifies the units from which a sample is selected.
Probability sampling gives units in the target population a known probability of selection.
Metadata explains the concepts, classifications, methods and quality information associated with a dataset.
Data visualization communicates patterns and trends through clear charts, tables and dashboards.
Responsible AI requires human oversight, accountability, transparency and attention to bias.
Python and pandas are useful for cleaning, transforming and analysing statistical datasets.
SQL supports structured data management through filtering, joins, grouping and aggregation.
